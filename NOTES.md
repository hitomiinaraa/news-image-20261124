A single page can hold a whole day if the ink is patient enough.
The headlines argue with each other before the coffee is poured.
Small type carries the weight the big type only announces.
A front page is a map of what the world chose to notice.
The fold hides the rest, but the rest still happened.
Every column ends where the next one begins to shout.
Tomorrow will wrap fish in this, and the day will still count.
The margins are where the quiet stories wait their turn.
